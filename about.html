About ScoreHub

Your front row seat to every match.

ScoreHub is a lightweight live-scores web app: football matches, standings, match timelines, F1 schedules — fast, free, and with no account required. It is a fan-built project and is not affiliated with any league, club or broadcaster.

Features

  • Live scores and fixtures across Europe's top leagues, with a match spotlight view.
  • Standings, match timelines, lineups and league browsing.
  • Formula 1 countdowns, results and standings.
  • Simulation Mode for exploring the interface when no matches are live.
  • Dark/light themes and favorites that stay in your own browser.

Data sources

Scores and fixtures come from ESPN, Formula 1 data from Jolpica, and league flags from Flagcdn. Team names and crests belong to their respective owners and are shown for identification only.

FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Your browser fetches scores directly from ESPN's public scoreboard endpoints and F1 data from Jolpica. See Privacy Policy for details.

What is Simulation Mode?

A built-in demo mode that generates realistic-looking matches so you can explore timelines, lineups and standings even when nothing is live. Toggle it from the API/Simulation switch on the homepage.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. The login form on the homepage is a demo placeholder.

Why is a match missing or delayed?

Coverage depends on the upstream providers. Lower-profile matches may lack lineups, events or live updates — and all scores can lag the real action by a minute or two.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet — but the site is designed mobile-first and works in any modern phone browser.
