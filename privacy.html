Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

ScoreHub is designed to work without accounts, tracking or sign-ups. This policy explains the limited data involved when you use the site.

1. What stays in your browser

Your preferences — such as theme choice and favorite teams — are stored only in your own browser's local storage. They are never sent to us, because we run no user accounts and no analytics backend.

2. Data fetched from third parties

Your browser connects directly to third-party data providers (including ESPN and Jolpica) to load scores and schedules. Those requests are subject to the providers' own privacy policies, and they may log standard technical information such as your IP address. We do not see or store that data.

3. What we don't collect

  • No accounts, names, emails or passwords.
  • No analytics cookies, advertising trackers or fingerprinting.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — we have none to share.

4. Contacting us

If you report an issue through our public repository (see Contact Us), whatever you choose to include in that public report will be visible to others. Please don't include sensitive personal information.

5. Children's privacy

ScoreHub is a general-audience scores site. We do not knowingly collect information from children, since we collect no user information at all.

6. Changes to this policy

If the site ever gains features that handle personal data (such as accounts or newsletters), this policy will be updated before those features launch. Continued use of the site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

7. Questions

See Contact Us, or read our Terms of Use.
